# Blurb
Nobody likes investigating a death during the festive season.

Nobody except for Luutkesr, that was. Luutkesr is a professional book reader with a boring personal life, a kleptomaniac brother, and a slothful sister. 

She is also an aspiring sleuth who takes the first chance she gets to investigate a murder. When one of the townspeople is found dead while reaching out for a fortune beyond anyone's dreams, Luutkesr is the only one who see beyond what's on the surface.

Will she become the detective she has always wanted to be, or will she remain a library employee with nothing better to do?


# Glossary
## Everything in order of appearance
## Names 
Veksr (Veck-Seer) -- A farmer.

Luutkesr (Lute-Kesser) -- The protagonist. Avid reader and official book reader for the library. Also a pastry addict.

Pemdr (Pem-Deer) -- Luutkesr's sister. A decent cook, but the single most laziest person in the village. If not the nation.

Buxingr (Books-In-Gear) -- Luutkesr's kleptomaniac brother.

Ruclengyr (Rook-Len-Gear) -- Luutkesr's adoptive father / mentor. Former Investigator (A sort of elite constable).

Old Man Kuvengyr (Ku-Ven-Gear) -- A man of myth who amassed ludicrous amounts of wealth over the course of his life. To escape from the nightmare that is taxes, he dug various pits throughout the world and buried his special coins in them. But full of hubris and greed was he, as the coins he buried were ones he minted and thus, not spendable.

## Terms
Avandr -- Avandr is a local month-long celebration typically held in the last month of winter. It celebrates gift-giving and the forgiveness of debts, both tenets represented by a creature of the town's folklore; The Fuglef, a humanoid figure with the head of a bird with antlers.

First Bell (And it's mirror, Fourth Bell) -- It is one of the three bells that ring at the start of each new day. Since the sun does not set, people rely on these bells to tell the time. Laborers, common workers, and soldiers wake up at First Bell. People with less physical works (shopkeepers, accountants, etc.) wake at Second Bell. Everyone else wakes at Third Bell.

Cycle -- It is the unit of time measurement, with three Bells for each Cycle, and 2 Cycles for each day.

Dwarf -- They are similar to Humans, but are shorter and have skin that resembles rocks and hair that resembles gems. Of course, they are not literally made of rocks, their skin is just a but rougher and tougher than regular Human skin.

Human -- Humans are what we know them to be, except all of them are dark-skinned here.

Green Man/Woman -- Green People are sentient masses of plants, thorns, flowers and general greenery in the shape of people.
## Establishments
The Pinvixr Brothers (Pin-Vix-Eer) -- One of a few coffee houses in the village owned and ran by their namesake, two twins.

Creamy Cups (You know how to pronounce this one) -- This one is a another coffee house owned by the Green Man, Prohubdis. It's notable for it's special (and only) product; *Coffee, creamed.*
